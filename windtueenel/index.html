📐 Aerodynamic Governing Equations

Lift Force (L) = CL * (1/2) * ρ * V² * A

Drag Force (D) = CD * (1/2) * ρ * V² * A

Lift Coefficient (CL) = L / (0.5 * ρ * V² * A)

Drag Coefficient (CD) = D / (0.5 * ρ * V² * A)

ρ = 1.225 kg/m³ (sea level), V = 28 m/s (test section), A = reference area (0.45 m²)
